NASHA'S BATTLE BACKDROPS: FREE HAUNTED BATTLES
==============================================

Hi, and welcome! This pack is free.

Inside you'll find 4 pixel art battle backgrounds for turn-based RPGs:

  haunted_graveyard  a moonlit graveyard with blank tombstones, twisted
                     trees and a stone crypt
  cursed_swamp       a dusky swamp with twisted mossy trees, dark pools
                     and pale mushrooms
  haunted_hall       a haunted manor hall with dusty curtains, empty
                     frames, a staircase and candelabras
  pumpkin_field      a Halloween pumpkin field with jack-o-lanterns,
                     corn stalks and an old barn

Moonlit blues, swamp greens, candle gold and pumpkin orange. Nothing in
the pack carries letters or numbers (every sign and board is blank), so
it fits a game in any language.


WHERE THINGS ARE
----------------

  640x360/      the native size: one PNG per background
  1280x720/     the same backgrounds scaled 2x (nearest neighbour)
  1920x1080/    the same backgrounds scaled 3x (nearest neighbour)

Every background is a full, opaque screen. The lower part is open,
flat ground, so your heroes and enemies have room to stand in front of
the scenery.


USING THE BACKGROUNDS
---------------------

Draw the background first, then your fighters, effects and battle menu
on top. Pick the folder that matches your screen size, or take the
640x360 files and scale them yourself.

Scale by whole numbers - 2x, 3x, 4x - and turn off smoothing so the
pixels stay crisp:

  Godot       set the texture Filter to Off (or Nearest)
  Unity       Filter Mode: Point, Compression: None
  GameMaker   untick "Interpolate colours between pixels"

For a 1280x720 or 1920x1080 game, the ready-made 2x and 3x files are
already pixel perfect.
